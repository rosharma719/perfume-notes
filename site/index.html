How this was made

Lift is how many times more often two notes appear together than if each were scattered independently. 1× is chance, 3× is three times as often. With several notes selected, it compares a note’s share among matching perfumes with its share overall.

Cleaning. 394 raw labels were merged into 153. Origins were dropped (Jasmine (Egypt) becomes Jasmine), aroma-chemicals folded into the note they imitate (Galaxolide (Musk) becomes Musk), and synonyms joined. Family colours are hand-assigned.

Source. The open doevent/perfume dataset, a 2024 snapshot, not a direct Fragrantica export. Note lists are flat, with no top, heart or base. Mass-market ranges are heavily represented, so “common” leans clean, sweet and musky. Tables ignore anything under about 40 perfumes.
